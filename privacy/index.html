    0. The short version

    Myth Notch has no account, no server and no telemetry. We — the developer — do not receive, store, see or process your notes, your usage, your device identifiers, your IP address, or anything else. There is no backend to send data to.

    Everything the app stores, it stores on your own device, encrypted with a key derived from a recovery phrase that only you hold. Network features (cloud sync, LAN sync) exist, are off by default, and when you turn one on, your data goes to a destination you chose — never to us.

    The rest of this document explains that in the detail that data-protection law, Google Play and the Apple App Store require.

    • other applications in the Myth ecosystem, which have their own policies;
    • third-party services you may choose to connect the app to (see §6);

In data-protection terms, this is analogous to a word processor installed on your computer. Where you configure the app to send encrypted data to a third-party service (§6), you determine that destination; that provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to what they hold.

    3. What the app stores on your device

    All of the following is stored in the app's private storage area on your device (the vault) and never leaves it unless you explicitly enable a sync or export feature.

    3.1 Encrypted with AES-256-GCM

    • Note content — text blocks, headings, lists, checkboxes, code blocks, tables, and the internal links between notes. One encrypted file per note.
    • Attachments — images you attach and voice recordings you make, each stored as a separate encrypted file.
    • The search index — a local database containing note titles, tag names, dates, word counts and note body text for search. The database file itself is encrypted at rest (SQLite3MultipleCiphers). It is derived data: deleting it is always safe, and it is never synced.
    • Sync configuration and bookkeeping — including any WebDAV server address and credentials you enter, and Google Drive tokens, if you enable those features.
    • Your custom note templates.

    3.2 Held in the operating system's secure store

    • The vault master key and your 12-word recovery phrase, kept in the platform secure store (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain / Windows DPAPI / macOS Keychain / Linux libsecret) so the app can unlock at launch and re-display your Login Key when you ask for it.
    • Optionally, the note-lock key, if you enable biometric quick-unlock for locked notes.

    3.3 Stored as plain (non-secret) local files

    These deliberately contain no note content and no keys:

    • Preferences — theme, language, sync mode, sync interval, active workspace, screen-protection toggles, and a locally generated device name/identifier used to distinguish your own devices from each other during sync.
    • A vault identity record — a version number and a cryptographic verifier used only to detect a mistyped recovery phrase. It contains no key and no phrase.
    • Workspace names/colours and, on desktop, sticky-note window positions.
    • Wrapped key files for the optional hidden vault and per-note lock. These contain keys encrypted under your own passphrase — not usable without it.

    3.4 Retention and deletion

    • Deleted notes go to Trash and are permanently purged after 30 days; you can purge them sooner.
    • You may delete any individual note, attachment, tag or workspace at any time.
    • Uninstalling the app removes the vault and the app's secure-store entries from that device, subject to your OS's behaviour. This is irreversible — there is no server-side copy.
    • The vault is excluded from operating-system cloud backup (allowBackup="false" and data-extraction rules on Android; the "exclude from backup" flag on iOS/macOS), so your vault is not silently copied into Google Drive or iCloud. Backups happen only when you create one yourself (§6.4).

    6. Optional features that involve a network or a third party

    All of the following are off by default. Enabling one is an explicit choice, made in Settings, with the consequences shown in the app.

    6.1 WebDAV sync

    If you configure a WebDAV server (for example your own Nextcloud), the app uploads your encrypted note files, attachments and configuration to the address you supplied. The app never decrypts data for transmission — the server operator sees ciphertext.

    However, the server operator (and, on an unencrypted connection, any network observer) can see metadata: file names, file sizes, timestamps, how often you sync, and your account name. To protect this, the app requires an https:// URL and only permits plain http:// to a local-network address (or via an explicit, off-by-default setting that states the risk in plain language). Your WebDAV credentials are stored encrypted in the vault and sent to that server only.

    You choose the server. Its operator's privacy policy governs what they do with what they hold. We have no relationship with it.

    6.2 Google Drive sync

    If you enable Google Drive sync, the app authenticates you with Google and uploads encrypted files to Google Drive's App Data folder — a hidden, per-application area that is invisible in your normal Drive interface and inaccessible to other apps. The app requests only the drive.appdata scope; it cannot see, read or modify any other file in your Drive.

    Google receives, and processes under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy): your Google account identity, the encrypted file contents, file names, sizes, and sync timestamps. We receive nothing. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on your device.

    6.3 Peer-to-peer LAN sync

    Devices on the same local network discover each other via mDNS/Bonjour (service type _notch-sync._tcp), prove to each other that they hold the same vault key, and exchange encrypted data directly. Nothing passes through any server, including ours. Other devices on the same network can observe that a device is advertising the service and that a connection is taking place, but cannot read the content.

    6.4 Manual backup (.mythbak)

    "Export backup" creates a single archive file containing your already-encrypted vault files. Because the contents are ciphertext, the archive is encrypted at rest — but it can only be restored with your recovery phrase, and wherever you save or send that file is your choice and your responsibility.

    6.5 Exporting or sharing an individual note

    Exporting a note to Markdown or PDF, or sharing it via the OS share sheet or clipboard, produces a decrypted, plaintext copy at a location of your choosing, outside the app's protection. This is a deliberate, explicitly requested exception to the "no plaintext on disk" rule. Once exported, the file is an ordinary file: it may be backed up by your OS, indexed by your device's search, or read by other apps.

    6.6 Voice transcription

    Transcription of a finished voice memo is handed to your operating system's own speech-recognition service (Android's speech recognizer, Apple's SFSpeechRecognizer). The app does not operate a transcription service and sends nothing to us.

    An honest limitation: whether that OS service processes audio entirely on-device or sends it to the vendor's servers is determined by your operating system, device and settings, not by this app. On many Android devices the default recognizer is Google's and may use the network unless an offline language pack is installed; Apple's recognizer may use server-based recognition unless on-device dictation is available. If this matters to your threat model, leave transcription off — it is off by default — or verify your OS's offline speech settings.

    6.7 Read-aloud (text-to-speech)

    Reading mode's "read aloud" passes note text to your operating system's text-to-speech engine. The same caveat as §6.6 applies: some OS TTS voices are network-backed.

    6.8 Android share-sheet capture and home-screen widget

    The app can receive text or images shared to it from other apps, and offers a home-screen shortcut into quick capture. Data flows into the app only, when you send it there.

    6.9 Updates and app stores

    If you installed the app from Google Play or the App Store, that store collects installation, purchase and crash data under its own policy, independently of us. We do not integrate with any store analytics.

    7. Encryption, your recovery phrase, and what we cannot do

    • Every note, attachment and the search index are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, with a fresh random nonce per write.
    • The master key is derived from your 12-word recovery phrase using Argon2id. The phrase is your identity: typing it on another device reconstructs the same vault. There is no account and no key escrow.
    • Optional layers use their own separate keys: the hidden vault and each decoy layer have their own independently derived keys and their own recovery codes; the per-note lock uses one separate credential shared across locked notes.

    We hold no copy of any key or phrase, and there is no recovery mechanism, backdoor or master key.

    If you lose your recovery phrase, your notes are permanently and irreversibly unreadable — by you, by us, and by anyone else. The same applies, per layer, to a hidden vault whose phrase and one-time recovery code are both lost. We cannot help. This is not a policy choice we could reverse on request; it is a property of the design.

    Store your recovery phrase somewhere safe and offline. Do not photograph it on a device that syncs to the cloud. The Login Key screen blocks screenshots for this reason.

    8. Optional privacy layers, and their honest limits

    The app offers protections beyond baseline encryption. Each is described here together with what it does not protect against.

    Hidden vault. A separate, opt-in encrypted vault with no visible entry point, intended for a threat model in which the existence of protected content should not be provable. It is opened by typing its phrase into the ordinary composer.

    Decoy layers. Up to two additional phrases, each opening a different set of notes, intended for coercion scenarios. A decoy can never reach a higher layer, because the higher layer's keys are not mathematically derivable from it.

    Per-note lock. A visible lock on individual notes, with one shared passphrase or pattern, protecting against a casual glance at an unlocked device.

    Screen protection. Blocking screenshots, screen recording and app-switcher thumbnails, configurable per content type.

    What these do not protect against

    We state these plainly rather than let marketing imply otherwise:

    • A compromised device. Malware, spyware, a rooted/jailbroken device, or an attacker with your unlocked device and your phrase defeats every layer. Encryption protects data at rest, not a machine already under someone else's control.
    • Metadata. Sync providers and network observers can see file sizes, counts and timing even though contents are ciphertext.
    • Forensic examination. The design avoids obvious traces, but we make no guarantee that a determined forensic examination of your device, its filesystem, its backups, its memory or its wear-levelled storage cannot infer the presence of additional data. Deniability is a design goal, not a mathematical promise.
    • iOS screenshots. Apple provides no way for an app to block a screenshot; on iOS only the app-switcher thumbnail can be protected. The app tells you this in-app rather than implying otherwise.
    • Anything outside the vault. Exported files, screenshots you take, text you copy to the clipboard, and OS-level dictation or TTS are outside the app's control.
    • Legal compulsion. See §7 of the Terms of Service.

    No software can promise perfect security. The app is engineered carefully and its code is open to inspection, but it is provided without warranty (see the Terms of Service).

    • Uygulamanın kesintisiz, hatasız veya kusur/zafiyet içermeyecek şekilde çalışacağını;
    • şifrelemesinin bugün veya gelecekte — gelecekteki kriptanaliz ya da kuantum gelişmeleri dahil — kırılamaz olduğunu;
    • verilerin kaybolmayacağını, bozulmayacağını veya okunamaz hâle gelmeyeceğini;
    • senkronizasyonun doğru, sırasıyla veya çakışmasız tamamlanacağını;
    • Uygulamanın, kaybı ya da ifşası esaslı zarar doğuracak bilgileri saklamak için uygun olduğunu.

    Uygulama; ölüm, yaralanma veya ağır malî, hukuki ya da çevresel zarara yol açabilecek hiçbir bağlam için sertifikalı değildir ve böyle bağlamlarda kullanılmamalıdır — tıbbi, hukuki delil niteliğinde, malî kayıt, güvenlik açısından kritik veya mevzuata uyum amaçlı kullanımlar dahil.

    Bazı ülkeler belirli garantilerin dışlanmasına izin vermez; o ülkelerde yukarıdaki hükümler yalnızca izin verilen ölçüde uygulanır ve ek haklarınız olabilir.

    12. Sorumluluğun sınırlandırılması

    Yürürlükteki hukukun izin verdiği azami ölçüde:

    1. Uygulamadan veya bu Şartlardan doğan ya da bunlarla ilgili dolaylı, arızi, özel, netice kabilinden, ibret verici veya cezai zararlardan, ayrıca veri kaybı, kâr kaybı, iş kaybı, itibar kaybı veya ikame mal/hizmet maliyetlerinden — hangi sorumluluk teorisine dayanırsa dayansın (sözleşme, haksız fiil, ihmal, kusursuz sorumluluk, kanun veya başka) ve böyle bir zarar olasılığı bize bildirilmiş olsa dahi — sorumlu değiliz.
    2. Uygulamadan veya bu Şartlardan doğan toplam sorumluluğumuz, (a) talebe konu olayın öncesindeki on iki ay içinde Uygulama için bize fiilen ödediğiniz tutar ile (b) {{LIABILITY_CAP}} tutarından yüksek olanını aşamaz. Uygulama ücretsizdir; çoğu durumda bu tutar sıfırdır.
    3. Bu sınırlamalar özellikle bir kurtarma ifadesinin veya kimlik bilgisinin kaybı, §5'in her türlü sonucu ve §7'de anlatılan özelliklerle korunan verinin ifşası ya da tespiti bakımından geçerlidir.

    Bu Şartlardaki hiçbir hüküm şunlara ilişkin sorumluluğumuzu dışlamaz veya sınırlamaz: ihmalimizden kaynaklanan ölüm veya bedensel zarar; hile veya hileli beyan; ağır ihmal veya kasıt; ya da hukuken dışlanması veya sınırlandırılması mümkün olmayan diğer her türlü sorumluluk. Tüketici iseniz, ikamet ettiğiniz ülkenin tüketici mevzuatından ve Türkiye bakımından 6502 sayılı Tüketicinin Korunması Hakkında Kanun'dan doğan emredici kanuni haklarınız bu Şartlardan etkilenmez ve yukarıdaki sınırlamalar yalnızca o hukukun izin verdiği ölçüde uygulanır.

    13. Tazmin

    Hukukun izin verdiği ölçüde, sizin Uygulamayı bu Şartlara aykırı biçimde ya da herhangi bir hukuk kuralını veya üçüncü kişi hakkını ihlal ederek kullanmanızdan doğan her türlü talep, zarar, sorumluluk veya masrafa (makul avukatlık ücretleri dahil) karşı {{PUBLISHER_NAME}}'i ve katkı sağlayanları tazmin etmeyi ve zarardan korumayı kabul edersiniz. Bu hüküm, kendi ağır ihmalimizden veya kastımızdan doğan taleplere ve tüketiciler bakımından ilgili tüketici mevzuatı uyarınca icra edilemez olduğu ölçüde uygulanmaz.

    14. Sona erme

    Bu Şartlar, Uygulamayı kullandığınız sürece geçerlidir. Uygulamayı kaldırarak istediğiniz an sona erdirebilirsiniz. Bu Şartları esaslı biçimde ihlal etmeniz hâlinde §3'teki lisans kendiliğinden sona erer. 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13 ve 15. bölümler sona ermeden sonra da yürürlükte kalır.

    15. Genel hükümler

    Değişiklikler. Uygulama veya hukuk değiştiğinde bu Şartları değiştirebiliriz. "Son güncelleme" tarihi güncel sürümü yansıtır; önceki sürümler projenin herkese açık geçmişinde kalır. Esaslı değişiklikler yürürlüğe girmeden önce Uygulama içinde veya sürüm notlarında duyurulur. Değişiklikten sonra kullanmaya devam etmek kabul anlamına gelir; kabul etmiyorsanız kullanmayı bırakın.
